Radio procedure

Communication standards for your community roleplay sessions.

📻 Clear radio traffic

Good radio communication is calm, brief and understandable. Follow your server's own radio codes and channel rules; codes differ between communities and real departments.

Suggested roleplay call structure

  1. Identify the unit or callsign.
  2. State who you are calling.
  3. Give a short, relevant status update.
  4. Wait for acknowledgement when required by your server.
Example (fictional):
“Engine 12 to Dispatch, we are responding to the training scenario.”

Radio etiquette

  • Do not talk over another active transmission.
  • Keep messages relevant to the incident.
  • Use plain language if a code is unclear.
  • Never use the radio to harass or argue with other players.

Scene safety

Roleplay awareness and safe teamwork.

🦺 Be aware of the scene

In a roleplay environment, scene safety means following the scenario leader's instructions, keeping the area organised and avoiding actions that disrupt other players.

Before joining a scenario

  • Listen to the briefing and understand your assigned role.
  • Use only the vehicles and equipment permitted by the server.
  • Keep clear of other players' active scenes unless assigned.
  • Ask a supervisor if instructions are unclear.
Important: This is game/community guidance only. Real emergencies require following official training and instructions from qualified emergency services.

Apparatus & equipment

Basic unit awareness for roleplay.

🚒 Know your assigned unit

Use the apparatus assigned by your training officer or scenario organiser. Each server may have its own vehicle names, loadouts and operating rules.

Good roleplay habits

  • Check that you have selected the correct vehicle for your assigned role.
  • Do not take another member's unit without permission.
  • Keep vehicles parked sensibly when not in use.
  • Follow server-specific rules for lights, sirens and vehicle movement.
  • Report missing or broken game assets to staff.
